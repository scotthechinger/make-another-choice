Shelby County

Every year, Shelby County decides where $507 million goes.

It is published in a budget book hundreds of pages long. It is entirely public. Almost nobody reads it.

Keep scrolling.

41.8%
of the general fund goes to the Sheriff

More than forty cents of every dollar.

$212 million out of a $507.5 million general fund. Of that, $70.3 million runs the jail — and another $45.9 million runs the Corrections Center on a separate fund.

The county says it wants to shrink the jail.

Written into Shelby County’s own FY2026 budget book, in the county’s own words:

“Support effective jail diversion programs for adult non-violent offenders with substance abuse or mental health issues to reduce detention costs and promote rehabilitation.”

“Reduce the jail population by reforming the bail system.”
